## **Objective**
The SAVYMINDS Platform aims to revolutionize executive reporting at Verizon by leveraging GenAI to automate data analysis, generate actionable insights, and deliver these insights in accessible formats such as newsletters, podcasts, and quarterly reviews.

---

## **Components of the Platform**

### **1. Data Sources**
The foundation of the reporting system, collecting raw data from:
- **CRM Systems**: Tracks customer interactions and service records.
- **POS Data**: Logs retail and online sales.
- **Billing Systems**: Provides financial data, including revenue and service charges.
- **Customer Surveys**: Captures qualitative feedback on customer experiences.

### **2. Data Processing**
The transformation of raw data into usable formats:
- **Data Extraction**: Retrieves structured and unstructured data from multiple systems.
- **Data Cleaning**: Removes errors, duplicates, and inconsistencies for accurate analysis.
- **Data Aggregation**: Combines data from multiple sources into meaningful datasets.

### **3. Report Generation**
Translating processed data into actionable insights:
- **KPI Calculation**:
  - Metrics like Gross Adds, Churn, ARPU, and Equipment Margin are calculated to track business performance.
- **Trend Analysis**:
  - Identifies patterns in customer behavior, sales performance, and regional growth.
- **Anomaly Detection**:
  - Flags unusual events, such as spikes in churn or drops in Net Adds, for proactive resolution.

### **4. AI-Driven Outputs**
Delivering insights in innovative, accessible formats:
- **Weekly Newsletters**:
  - AI-generated emails summarizing key takeaways from the week’s performance.
  - Example: _“Net Adds increased by 7% this week, driven by a promotional campaign in the Midwest.”_
- **Monthly Podcasts**:
  - 5-10 minute audio summaries of monthly performance, accessible on mobile or desktop.
  - Example: _“ARPU rose by 3%, contributing $10M in additional revenue this month.”_
- **Quarterly Deep Dives**:
  - In-depth audio or text reports summarizing trends, challenges, and strategic insights.

| **Net Adds**            | Total activations minus deactivations.                                                         |
| **Churn**               | Percentage of customers lost.                                                                  |
| **ARPU**                | Average revenue per user.                                                                      |
| **Equipment Margin**    | Profitability from device sales.                                                               |

## **Analyzing ROI for the SAVYMINDS Platform**

To assess the return on investment (ROI) for the SAVYMINDS Platform, focus on measurable outcomes in time savings, cost efficiency, and business impact.

### **1. Time Savings**
- **Metric**: Hours saved by automating data analysis, KPI calculation, and report generation.
- **Example**:
  - If 20 hours/week are saved across 10 BI team members, this equals 800 hours/month saved.
  - Value of time saved = 800 hours × average hourly rate.

### **2. Cost Efficiency**
- **Metric**: Reduction in manual reporting costs.
- **Example**:
  - A 30% reduction in manual efforts equates to significant savings in operational costs.

### **3. Business Impact**
- **Metric**: Improvement in key KPIs due to actionable insights.
- **Example**:
  - A churn reduction from 2% to 1.8% saves $500,000 in lost revenue, demonstrating a clear ROI.

### **4. Engagement Metrics**
- **Metric**: Stakeholder adoption of newsletters, podcasts, and dashboards.
- **Example**:
  - A 75% adoption rate among executives indicates strong engagement and alignment.

### **5. Strategic Value**
- **Metric**: Long-term benefits from data-driven decision-making.
- **Example**:
  - Enhanced forecasting accuracy contributes to better resource allocation and revenue growth.
