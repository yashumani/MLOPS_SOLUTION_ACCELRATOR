Two design rules to read first

Training starts from the raw split

Stage 2 creates raw training data and a locked final test. Candidate pipelines learn their own transformations inside evaluation folds.

S02 raw train→Phase A / B / C

S03 → S04 provides diagnostics and the training-only drift reference. Its transformed output does not feed model training.

Registration and promotion are separate

Final evaluation freezes one champion before opening the locked test. The exact fitted preprocessing and estimator are saved in a model bundle and validated for registration.

Quality decision→Exact bundle registry

The external controller may submit retraining candidates. Model promotion remains manual; there is no automatic serving-endpoint deployment in this graph.

Scope, evidence and boundaries

What is outside the active product?

Forecasting / time-series training, S00, S05t, S07 and S11 are unwired, legacy or inactive. Azure AutoML is not a supported discovery engine. The graph does not automatically promote models or deploy serving endpoints. Historical blueprints and old recipe counts do not override the current source contract.

What do the operational screens prove?

They implement job and artifact inspection, policy decisions and control actions. A reconstructed job timeline is not a live raw-log stream; a health field based on ML client construction is not Azure reachability proof; a planned schedule is not an active schedule. Source presence does not establish deployed service health.

How should I read the diagrams?

A phase groups related work: Phase A discovers baselines, Phase B searches recipes, and Phase C tunes a candidate. A model bundle includes the fitted data transformations and estimator. S14 only produces a decision artifact; an external controller can act on approved evidence. Use the source-coverage guide for detail beyond the visual maps.
